\# Population data sources (MP1)



All files downloaded 15 October 2026 and stored unmodified in this folder.

Publisher: Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS), https://www.ubos.org



\## 1. Census population counts and mid-year projections, 2015–2021

\- File: Census\_Population\_counts\_(2002\_and\_2014)\_by\_Region,\_District\_and\_Mid-Year\_Population\_projections\_(2015-2021).xlsx

\- URL: https://www.ubos.org/wp-content/uploads/statistics/Census\_Population\_counts\_(2002\_and\_2014)\_by\_Region,\_District\_and\_Mid-Year\_Population\_projections\_(2015-2021).xlsx

\- Listed on UBOS Population \& Censuses page as "Last Updated on 25th June 2023".

\- Used: sheet "Sheet3", rows Jinja and Busia, years 2015–2021 (persons).

\- Notes: 2015–2021 values are projections from the 2014 census, not counts.

&#x20; No separate Jinja City row; Jinja figures cover the pre-2020 undivided area.

&#x20; Some other rows show apparent errors (e.g. Kaberamaido and Mbarara 2021 jumps,

&#x20; Butebo 2015 below its 2014 census count); Jinja and Busia rows are internally consistent.
